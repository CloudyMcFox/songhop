Open any music link in your music app.

A friend sends you a Spotify link but you use Apple Music, or the other way round. Songhop finds the same song, album or artist and opens it in the app you use.

How to use it

Share sheet. Long-press a music link in Messages or any app, tap Share, then tap the Songhop icon. If you don't see it, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More and add it.

Paste. Copy a link, open Songhop and tap Paste.

Shortcuts and Siri. In the Shortcuts app, search for Songhop and use Open Music Link in My App.

Common questions

Which services work? Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, Amazon Music, Deezer and SoundCloud, in any direction.

It opened a search instead of the song. Songhop couldn't find an exact match, so it searched your app for the song name. Please report the link so the matching can be improved.

How do I change my music app? Open Songhop and tap the music app button in the top-right corner.
