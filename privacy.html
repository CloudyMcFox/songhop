Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

In short: Songhop doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The links you convert are used only to find the matching song and aren't stored.

What happens when you convert a link

To find the same song in your music app, Songhop sends the shared link, or the song, album or artist name and artist read from it, to:

  • The Songhop server, which runs on Cloudflare. It looks the song up with Spotify's search and passes back the result. It doesn't keep a record of the links you convert or who converted them. It may briefly cache a lookup result so the same song is faster next time, but the cache isn't connected to who asked.
  • The music service the link came from, such as Tidal or Spotify. Songhop opens the shared link's public page to read the song's name.
  • Apple Music and Deezer's public search, to find a direct link.

Like any website you visit, these services can see your IP address when your device contacts them. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with it.

Rate limiting

Songhop creates a random ID when you install it. It isn't linked to you, your Apple account or your device, and it's sent to the Songhop server only so one person can't overload the service. The server uses it for about a minute to count requests and doesn't store it.

Data kept on your device

Your chosen music app and your conversion history are saved only on your iPhone. You can clear the history in the app at any time, and deleting Songhop removes everything.

Children

Songhop doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
